PROVENANCE & LIMITATIONS

Real data. Visible limits.

Route: OpenStreetMap relation 3094865, version 116; snapshot August 29, 2022, mirrored by superr0ute/traildb. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The relation is disconnected near Ward Creek. No connector has been invented.

Elevation & contours: Mapzen / Tilezen Terrarium terrain via the same repository’s cached zoom-11 tiles. ~60 m pixels at Tahoe. Elevations use the snapshot’s DEM samples, a 120 m triangular smoothing window, and 30 m profile spacing. Gains and steepness are estimates at this resolution.

Waypoints: 121 OSM features from the repository’s August 2022 POI export. Camping permission, trailhead coverage, water reliability, access routes, and current conditions are not verified.

Terrain: Local hillshade, hypsometric tint, and 100 m contours derived from 13 terrain tiles. USGS / Mapzen / Tilezen / upstream provider credits apply. Exact upstream acquisition dates and per-tile provenance headers are unavailable in this mirror.
